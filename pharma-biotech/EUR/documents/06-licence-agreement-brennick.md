Licence agreement between Calvera Pharmaceuticals B.V. and Brennick Biologics (synthetic) for PX-330.
---page---
7.1 Calvera Pharmaceuticals B.V. shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) a milestone payment of EUR 11,040,000 upon the first dosing of a patient in a Phase 2 trial of PX-330.
7.2 Calvera Pharmaceuticals B.V. shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) a milestone payment of EUR 23,000,000 upon the start of the first Phase 3 trial of PX-330.
8.1 Calvera Pharmaceuticals B.V. shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) a royalty of 6% of net sales of PX-330.
8.4 Calvera Pharmaceuticals B.V. shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) an annual licence fee of EUR 230,000.
