Licence agreement between Calvera Pharmaceuticals Ltd and Brennick Biologics (synthetic) for PX-330.
---page---
7.1 Calvera Pharmaceuticals Ltd shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) a milestone payment of GBP 9,480,000 upon the first dosing of a patient in a Phase 2 trial of PX-330.
7.2 Calvera Pharmaceuticals Ltd shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) a milestone payment of GBP 19,750,000 upon the start of the first Phase 3 trial of PX-330.
8.1 Calvera Pharmaceuticals Ltd shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) a royalty of 6% of net sales of PX-330.
8.4 Calvera Pharmaceuticals Ltd shall pay Brennick Biologics (synthetic) an annual licence fee of GBP 197,500.
