Lease agreement for Unit 11 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Inc. as landlord and Gullwing Robotics (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 November 2023 to 31 October 2033.
The initial base rent is USD 8,200.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 4.15% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
A leasing commission of USD 64,000.00 for this lease remains unpaid to Harbourline Realty (synthetic).
