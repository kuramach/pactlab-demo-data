Lease agreement for Unit 3 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Inc. as landlord and Brightwater Analytics (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 31 December 2029.
The initial base rent is USD 15,000.00 per month.
Base rent is fixed for the term.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 8.03% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
