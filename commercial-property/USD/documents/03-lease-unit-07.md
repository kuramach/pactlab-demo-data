Lease agreement for Unit 7 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Inc. as landlord and Northlight Studios (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 31 January 2027.
The initial base rent is USD 6,000.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 3.32% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
