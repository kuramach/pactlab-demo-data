Lease agreement for Unit 10 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates B.V. as landlord and Haldon Insurance Brokers (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 28 February 2029.
The initial base rent is EUR 6,348.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 3.05% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
