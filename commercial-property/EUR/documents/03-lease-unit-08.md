Lease agreement for Unit 8 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates B.V. as landlord and Fernbank Foods (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 30 June 2032.
The initial base rent is EUR 9,936.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 5.82% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
