Lease agreement for Unit 6 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates B.V. as landlord and Teal Harbor Software (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 31 May 2028.
The initial base rent is EUR 8,740.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 4.43% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
