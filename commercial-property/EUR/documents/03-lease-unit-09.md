Lease agreement for Unit 9 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates B.V. as landlord and Cinder Path Fitness (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 30 November 2026.
The initial base rent is EUR 4,784.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 2.49% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
