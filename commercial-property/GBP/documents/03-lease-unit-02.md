Lease agreement for Unit 2 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Ltd as landlord and Pellham Engineering (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 31 March 2031.
The initial base rent is GBP 15,800.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 2% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 11.36% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
The landlord shall pay a tenant improvement allowance of GBP 237,000.00, of which GBP 142,200.00 remains unpaid.
