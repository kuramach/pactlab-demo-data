Lease agreement for Unit 5 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Ltd as landlord and Moorland Medical Supplies (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 30 September 2030.
The initial base rent is GBP 9,480.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 6.65% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
