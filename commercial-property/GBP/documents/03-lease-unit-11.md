Lease agreement for Unit 11 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Ltd as landlord and Gullwing Robotics (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 November 2023 to 31 October 2033.
The initial base rent is GBP 6,478.00 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 4.15% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
A leasing commission of GBP 50,560.00 for this lease remains unpaid to Harbourline Realty (synthetic).
