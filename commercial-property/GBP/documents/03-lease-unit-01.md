Lease agreement for Unit 1 at Quayside Business Park between Quayside Estates Ltd as landlord and Corvane Logistics (synthetic) as tenant.

The term runs from 1 July 2021 to 30 June 2027.
The initial base rent is GBP 54,865.50 per month.
Base rent increases by 3% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
The lease is triple net: the tenant pays its proportionate share of 45.71% of the park's recoverable operating expenses, real estate taxes and insurance.
